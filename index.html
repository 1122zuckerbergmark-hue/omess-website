About Our School

Oppong Memorial Senior High School (OMESS) is a co-educational Category B institution located in Kokofu within the Bekwai Municipal Assembly of the Ashanti Region, Ghana.

The school operates as both a boarding and day facility.

Established around the 1973/1974 academic year through the vision of Nana Aduboahene and other influential citizens.
